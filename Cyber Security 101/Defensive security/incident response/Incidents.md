security solution finds an event or group of events associated with a possible harmful activity, it triggers an alert

alerts that point to something dangerous but are not harmful are referred to as false positives.

alerts that point to something harmful and are actually dangerous are called true positives.

true positive alerts are sometimes referred to as **Incidents**.

Incidents can be categorized as low, medium, high, or critical based on the impact they can create.
